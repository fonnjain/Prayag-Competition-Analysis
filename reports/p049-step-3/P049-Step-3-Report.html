1. Loader rule as built

Existing product: prices only. The loader never writes Category, Range or division for an existing product. All existing catalogue fields, including names and lifecycle state, remain untouched. File differences are informational: classification differs — not changed, with both values.
New product outside the three exact Range divisions: master series → category; series_range is omitted.
New CP/PTMT product without Category: new CP/PTMT product needs a category: create it in the product editor. Only that product and its colour prices are refused; accepted rows remain eligible.

Classification is re-evaluated under a catalogue-membership lock at Apply. An existing product's exact stored item code is retained for its price rows. If eligible price-row count changes since preview, Apply rolls back and requires a fresh preview. An already-loaded identical source can now show a read-only preview; it cannot be staged or applied twice.

Original candidate rule: non-empty Category exactly equals stored Range, in one of the three exact approved divisions. The expanded files also include the explicitly identified active CP products with a capitalised range name in Category and empty Range, flagged capitalised range in category, Range empty. This is a separate review basis, not equality evidence and not a stored correction. Outside these three, Category = Range is correct under the master's meaning and is not treated as loader-copy evidence. Combined divisions are excluded from this review and remain in their own file. Nothing is normalized, merged or relabeled.

How looks_like was decided: an explicit semantic reading of the labels. Functional product groups, accessory groups and grade/thickness-only descriptions are category (e.g. Accessories, Ball Cocks, Waste Coupling). Named families, numbered collections and their colour variants are range (e.g. Marcus 68, Aspire 52). Allied is provisionally read as an accessory umbrella category; Standard handle generations and explicit numbered steel series are provisionally read as ranges. Each row includes its reason. These are review recommendations, not approval or stored corrections.
