5. Pipe identity: explicit limitation and future design

Development current matching rules and database check: 24 Sep 2026 20:56:01 IST.

0 / 272 locally assigned Apollo pipe rows can match directly from this PDF today. The stable V2 key uses sheet class, Prayag code and size. Apollo's PDF does not carry the Prayag code; the code-side PDF adapter holds all 272 as needing review rather than inferring a mapping. A new PDF therefore still needs the reviewed pairing workbook.

Design note only — not built: A second, supplier-side identity could normalize Apollo's own printed section, product description, material/variant qualifiers and ordered size. A reviewed one-time mapping from each such identity to the existing Prayag-anchored V2 identity would then allow later PDFs to propose matches. Before allowing automated approval, verify that each PDF identity is unique on the page and across periods, preserve semantic variants such as with/without Jali, adjudicate renamed labels and OCR uncertainty, and hold duplicate/ambiguous results for human review. Price equality must never resolve identity. Risks include collapsing two distinct products under the same text/size, swapped dimension order, heading drift, OCR mistakes, and a renamed or reformatted supplier catalogue.
