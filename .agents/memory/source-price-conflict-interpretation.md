Legacy fitting conflict flags are not automatically errors or product-identity
collisions. The user confirmed against the authoritative master that many
February-period flags compare an earlier "using" price with a 10 August 2026
revision recorded as the "other source value".

**Why:** Treating different revision prices as simultaneous competing prices
misrepresents legitimate price changes. A missing later-dated row does not by
itself prove today's quoted amount is old.

**How to apply:** Compare the current amount, explicit effective-date field and
revision status note independently. Preserve the existing conflict register;
read-only reports do not authorize flag clearing or price/history corrections.

The PEXI pipe per-metre versus per-pipe basis needs an explicit Prayag ruling.
Do not infer the price unit from the "6 METER" product name or automatically
multiply prices by six. Do not apply a separate PPR pipe unit decision to PEXI.

**Why:** The user explicitly reserves this ruling to Prayag; a length in a name
and differently priced source observations do not establish the quotation unit.

**How to apply:** Report stored periods, prices and source provenance unchanged.
Only a separately authorized supplier-confirmed unit decision can justify a
restatement or price adjustment.
