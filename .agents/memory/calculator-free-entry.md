---
name: Calculator assertions and scope boundary
description: Why sales quoting is unscoped and must never become stored pricing evidence.
---

Free-entry calculator prices are basket-local assertions, never market evidence
or catalogue corrections. There is no save/promote/register action, even for an
administrator. Do not write asserted products, brand names or prices into source
tables, coverage or analytics.

**Why:** Salespeople quote retailers' claims live in shops. A hand-entered price
can look exactly like an official one and be shown to a retailer as company data.
Promotion would require a separately approved review queue, attribution trail
and source-acceptance policy; the user explicitly deferred that work.

**How to apply:** Preserve the App / Edited / Entered vocabulary across cells,
summaries and exports, retain overridden app values, and visibly disclose every
asserted comparison. A deliberate one-off competitor is distinct from a stored
competitor with the same name. Item-code identity still preserves every
character; missing catalogue metadata is not permission to infer it.

The calculator deliberately searches all catalogue products and competitors
without company/personal bucket filtering. This is a quoting-only exception,
not a change to Price Finder or any analytical screen.

**Why:** A salesperson must quote whichever product or competitor the retailer
names, including ones absent from stored lists.

**How to apply:** Keep the unscoped quoting-aid disclosure. Keep Price Finder's
enforcement unchanged. Count ad-hoc products in coverage denominators while
restricting every brand's percentage to the products actually compared.

An exact catalogue identity stays a catalogue product even when it is inactive
or has no date-eligible app price. Preserve its real metadata; a basket-only
price is Entered, not evidence that the product is ad-hoc or reactivated.

**Why:** Full-catalogue shop quoting must distinguish a genuinely unknown SKU
from an existing catalogue record that the ordinary active-product finder
does not currently price.

**How to apply:** Never borrow a similar active code's price or revive
ineligible MRP evidence merely to make the quote work.
