# P009 — COMPETITION — Enable stage 2 with visible coverage limits

> **P009** · app: **COMPETITION** (`prayag-price.com` / `fonnjain/Prayag-Competition-Analysis`)
> **THIS PROMPT CHANGES CODE.** It is not a read-only query.
> Quote `P009` when reporting back.

Enable the margin and volume rules. The known data gaps do not block this — they reduce how many products can be priced, and the generator is already designed to exclude and explain rather than guess.

**A tool that prices 3,163 of 6,106 products and says why not the rest is more useful than no tool.** The gaps become visible constraints, not reasons to wait.

---

## 1. What the gaps actually are

Measured, not estimated:

| Gap | Size | Effect on the generator |
|---|---|---|
| Catalogue codes with no FY2026-27 sales | 2,943 of 6,106 | Excluded from volume rules; already designed |
| Sold codes not resolving to the catalogue | 814, ₹9.36 Cr | Not in scope — no price to suggest |
| Margin data ends | June 2026 | Margin rules run on Apr–Jun; state the age |
| PTMT margin held | Jan–Apr 2026 (H1) | Excluded; hold shown with its reason |
| FY2026-27 reconciliation | −₹10.8 L on ₹135.23 Cr | **0.08%** — see below |
| Codes awaiting Prayag confirmation | 10, ₹2.63 Cr | Excluded until confirmed |

**On the reconciliation gap.** It is 0.08% of the total, and the rules use **item-level margin percentages**, not the aggregate. A discrepancy of that size cannot move a product's margin percentage enough to change which side of a floor it falls. It must still be shown — see section 3 — but it is not a reason to hold the feature.

## 3. Coverage is part of the output, not a footnote

Every run shows what it could and could not reach, **before** the suggestions:

```
SCOPE
  6,106 catalogue products
  3,163 have FY2026-27 sales        volume rules apply
  2,943 have none                   excluded from volume rules
  2,421 have June 2026 margin       margin rules apply

DATA AGE
  Prayag MRP        current
  Sparsh Pearl      01 Jul 2026      2 months old
  Ashirvad          01 Mar 2025      18 MONTHS OLD
  Margin            Jun 2026         3 months old, latest available
  Sales             FY2026-27 Apr-Aug, provisional (open months)

KNOWN LIMITS IN EFFECT
  PTMT margin Jan-Apr 2026 held (H1) — excluded
  Sales register is Rs 10.8 L below control on Rs 135.23 Cr (0.08%)
  10 codes await Prayag confirmation — excluded, Rs 2.63 Cr of sales
```

Nobody should have to ask how complete a run was. **If a limit is not on that panel, it does not exist as far as the user is concerned** — and a limit discovered after a price has changed is worse than one that blocked the run.

---

## 4. Exclusions stay first-class

Already built, and must survive this change: an excluded product appears in the output **with its reason**, never silently absent and never substituted with an average.

Extend the reasons to cover the new cases:

```
no FY sales                 volume tier cannot be assigned
margin month held (H1)      margin floor cannot be evaluated
margin data ends Jun 2026   margin figure is 3 months old   [warn, not exclude]
code awaiting confirmation  sales attribution unresolved
```

The third is a **warning, not an exclusion**. Three-month-old margin is usable; eighteen-month-old competitor pricing is more questionable and is already flagged. Let the user decide, but make sure they are told.

---

## 5. One new rule worth having

**Confidence tiering.** Let a scenario be restricted to products where all inputs are present and current:

```
Run only where:
  [x] competitor price exists
  [x] competitor price is under 12 months old
  [x] FY sales data exists
  [x] margin data exists and is not held
```

This is the shape most users will actually want for a first run — price the products you can be confident about, then look at the rest separately. Without it, someone will run across the whole catalogue and then work out which suggestions to trust.

Report how many products pass all four filters.

## 6. Acceptance tests

```
1.  Margin floors and volume tiers can be saved and executed.
2.  A product with no FY sales is excluded from volume rules, with reason.
3.  A PTMT product in a held margin month is excluded, and the hold is named.
4.  A product whose margin is from Jun 2026 is included with an age warning,
    not excluded.
5.  The coverage panel appears before suggestions and states sales coverage,
    margin coverage, every data age and every known limit.
6.  The reconciliation variance is stated on the panel.
7.  The confidence filter restricts a run to products with all inputs present
    and current, and reports the count.
8.  The 10 codes awaiting confirmation are excluded and listed.
9.  Running the same scenario twice on the same data gives identical results.
10. No price is written by any action on this page.
```

Test 5 is the one that makes shipping with gaps defensible. Test 10 has not changed and must not.
