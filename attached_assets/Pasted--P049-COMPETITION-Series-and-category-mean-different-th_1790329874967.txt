# P049 — COMPETITION — Series and category mean different things depending on how a product arrived

> **P049** · app: **COMPETITION** (`prayag-price.com`)
> **READ-ONLY. Change nothing.** No commits. No data changes. No relabelling.
> Quote `P049` when reporting back.

P048 Step 2 surfaced this, and it outranks everything else in that report:

> *The normalized authoritative master writes its `series` value to **both `series_range` and `category`**; the generic importer treats `GROUP` as category and `RANGE` as series.*

**Those two paths disagree.** One duplicates a single value into series **and** category. The other splits `GROUP` and `RANGE` into category and series respectively.

So **`category` means a different thing depending on how the product arrived**, and nothing in the data says which.

Your own caveat is the correct stopping point and the most useful sentence in that report: *"I cannot assert that equivalence for every import path."* This prompt establishes the shape of it before anyone tries to fix it.

---

# Why this matters beyond tidiness

**It reaches back into the Uncategorized work.** P029, P035 and P036 took three prompts to clear 13 products with `category = NULL`, and the fix had to be applied twice because the first pass reached development only.

If `category` is sometimes a genuine category and sometimes a copy of series, then that vocabulary was never one thing. **A future cleanup built on the assumption that it is would make the data worse while appearing to tidy it.**

It also means **P048's Add Category and Add Series are feeding fields that partly overlap** — which is not a reason to undo either, but it is a reason to know the shape before more values are added.

# 2. How many products are in each state?

**2.1** Count products where `category` **equals** `series_range`.

**2.2** Count products where they **differ**, and both are non-null.

**2.3** Count products where `category` is null and `series_range` is not, and the reverse.

**2.4** Count products where both are null.

The four counts must sum to the catalogue total. Report the total and confirm they do.

**2.5** Do these counts split cleanly by how the product arrived — by `load_batch_id`, source, or creation path? **If the equal-value products are exactly those from the authoritative master, that confirms the mechanism.** If they do not split cleanly, something else is also writing these fields and that is a more interesting finding.

Report for **development and production separately**, each with its clock time. They diverge.

## What comes after

Once the shape is known, the decision is a business one: **are series and category two different things Prayag needs, or one thing recorded twice?**

- If two, the paths that conflate them are bugs and some products carry a category that is really a series.
- If one, the redundant field should stop being written and the screens should stop offering both.

**That ruling needs Prayag, not an agent** — exactly as the duplicate Prince codes did under P034. This prompt gives them the counts to rule on.

P048 Step 2 is **not blocked by this** and can publish. The series control is correct, the refusal works, and no product or price moved.
