## CORRECTION — page 98 must NOT be added to `tablePages`

The staged change adds the `TR-` entry to **both** `seriesMap` and `tablePages` in `sparsh-pearl.json`. Adding page 98 to `seriesMap` is correct. Adding it to `tablePages` is **wrong and will break the fix it is meant to deliver.**

Page 98 is a **grid page**, not a table page. Its items are printed as tiles:

```
TR-531   | MRP : ₹ 319/-
Soap Dish (Oval)
S. Box : 24 Pcs. | M. Box : 96 Pcs.
```

This is the same tile format used on the ED-/REG-/TS- series pages. The genuine table pages (83, 84, 96, 97) have real column headers — `Cat No. | Sizes | MRP in ₹ | S.Box (Pcs) | M.Box (Pcs)` — with one row per size. Page 98 has none of that.

If page 98 is treated as a table page, the extractor will look for column headers that do not exist and the four `TR-` items will be missed again.

**Change required:**
- **Keep** the `TR-` entry in `seriesMap` with `pages: [98]`.
- **Remove** `98` from `tablePages`. It must stay `[83, 84, 96, 97]`.

Page 98 also has **two sections** on the same page — `TRANSPARENT` (the `TR-` codes) and `P.T.M.T` (`BA-` codes). The extractor must read the whole page, not stop at the first section. The `BA-` codes are not mapped to Prayag and will correctly appear under "New products found".
