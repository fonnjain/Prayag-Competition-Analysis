# PART 2 — Which other categories are priced per metre?

This is the question P038 answered for PPR and nobody has answered for anything else.

## 2.1 Why it matters

A pipe sold in a 3-metre or 6-metre length has two possible prices, and they differ by 3× or 6×. If the application stores one basis and the competitor master quotes the other, **every comparison in that category is wrong by a factor of three or six** — and it will look like a plausible competitive position rather than an error.

PPR was found only because Prayag happened to send a file. That is not a detection method.

## 2.2 What is already known

```
category        length         master v3    production    status
PPR Pipe        3 MTR          per pipe     per pipe      corrected, verified
Casing Pipe     3 MTR          per pipe     per pipe      verified
UGD Pipe        6 MTR          per pipe     per pipe      verified
CPVC Pipe       3 M            ?            ?             NOT CHECKED
UPVC Pipe       3 M            ?            ?             NOT CHECKED
AGRI Pipe       6 METERS       ?            ?             NOT CHECKED
SWR Pipe        3 M and 6 M    ?            ?             NOT CHECKED
```

Roughly **375 products** sit in the four unchecked categories: CPVC Pipe 40, UPVC Pipe 52, AGRI Pipe 123, SWR Pipe 160.

**SWR Pipe is the one to look at hardest.** Its competitor master sheet carries both a `Length` and a `Length (Mtr)` column, and the range mixes 3-metre and 6-metre products. A category with two lengths cannot be checked with a single ratio.

## 2.3 What to report

For **each** of CPVC Pipe, UPVC Pipe, AGRI Pipe and SWR Pipe, in **production**:

1. The count of products and the count of `mrp_price_history` rows.
2. **For five products in each, spread across the size range:** the stored MRP, and the product's length if the name or size field states one.
3. Whether the stored value is per metre or per pipe. Evidence it: state the length, the stored value, and what the other basis would be.
4. **The same five against the competitor master**, which quotes per pipe for these categories — its sheets carry a single dated price column, unlike Casing, UGD and PPR which carry both.

If the stored value equals the competitor master's Prayag column, the basis agrees. If it is that value divided by 3 or 6, it does not.

5. For SWR Pipe specifically: report the 3-metre and 6-metre products **separately**. A single ratio across a mixed-length range proves nothing.

Then do the same five-product check in **development**, and say whether the two environments agree.

## 2.4 One more comparison worth making

For any category where the basis is **per metre** in the application:

- How many competitor comparisons does that category currently carry?
- What is the reported median gap today?

That is the size of the error, stated as the thing people are actually reading off the screen.
