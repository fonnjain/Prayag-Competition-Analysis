## 1. The source is unusually clean

Worth stating because it is not the norm here:

- **All 669 Prayag codes exist** in `catalog_products`. None missing.
- **All 669 Prayag prices in the source match ours exactly.** No reconciliation needed.
- **No duplicate codes** on either side — each Prayag code maps to one Ashirvad code and vice versa.

So the mapping can be trusted as given. Load it as-is.

---

## 2. Do not load the Prayag column

The file carries `prayag_mrp_reference` for context. **It must not be loaded.**

Prayag MRP comes from the authoritative master and is resolved by date. A competitor file supplying Prayag prices is exactly the confusion that has to be avoided — the competitor side and the Prayag side must never come from the same upload.

The `gap_pct` column is likewise for human review only. Gaps are computed at query time, both sides resolved at the same date.

---

## 3. Effective date, and why it needs flagging

Every Ashirvad price is dated **01 March 2025** — eighteen months old. Sparsh Pearl Vol 6.2 is dated 01 July 2026.

Load the date as given; it is what the source states. But **surface the age prominently** wherever Ashirvad appears — on the product page, in comparison, and in exports. The existing "valid from" treatment should already do this, but confirm it reads clearly when the date is this old.

A user comparing against an eighteen-month-old list should be able to see that at a glance, not work it out.

---

## 4. Coverage is partial, and that must be visible

Ashirvad covers **669 of 1,183** Prayag plumbing products — 57%. Coverage varies sharply by category:

```
CPVC Pipe        40 / 40      UPVC Fittings   154 / 269
CPVC Fittings   171 / 255     AGRI Pipe        67 / 123
SWR Fittings    121 / 149     SWR Pipe         56 / 160
UPVC Pipe        40 / 52      AGRI Fittings    20 / 135
```

A Prayag product with no Ashirvad mapping must show as **"no data"**, never as a zero gap or an absent competitor column that implies parity. This is the same rule already applied for Vectus.

---

## 5. Two competitors, not one

This is the first time the application holds more than one mapped competitor with real data. Check that everything built around a single competitor still holds:

- **Comparison** must show both, and the competitor filter must be genuinely multi-select rather than defaulting to Sparsh.
- **The Price Finder inline gap line** currently shows one competitor. Decide what it shows when a product has two — both, or the closest, stated explicitly.
- **Competition Analysis** rankings and opportunity/threat classification must account for two competitors, not silently pick one.
- **Exports** need a competitor column, or one column pair per competitor.

Report what currently assumes a single competitor. That list is more useful than the load itself.

## Worth knowing before anyone reads the numbers

Two figures will stand out, and both should be checked with the business rather than taken at face value.

**CPVC is not a close race.** Prayag is cheaper on 40 of 40 pipes and 161 of 171 fittings, median +43% to +50%. Against Sparsh the median is +2.9%. Either Ashirvad sits well above Prayag in CPVC, or their list is materially out of date.

**AGRI Pipe runs the other way.** Prayag is cheaper on only **3 of 67**, median −6.2%. It is the single category where Ashirvad undercuts consistently.

Both may be real. Both are also what an eighteen-month-old price list would look like. Flag them in the report rather than presenting them as settled findings.
