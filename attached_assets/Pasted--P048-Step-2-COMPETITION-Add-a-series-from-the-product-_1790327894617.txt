# P048 — Step 2 — COMPETITION — Add a series from the product editor

> **P048 Step 2** · app: **COMPETITION** (`prayag-price.com`)
> **THIS PROMPT CHANGES CODE.** One more vocabulary control.
> Quote `P048 Step 2` when reporting back.

P048 is live and working. **Create New Product, Step 2** has a **Series** field, auto-populated from existing values, with no way to add a new one.

Add it — reusing what P048 already built. Three things differ from Division and Category, and they are the substance of this prompt.

---

# 1. Reuse, do not rebuild

**1.1** The Add Series control uses **the same normalisation, refusal, reason and provenance machinery** as Add Division and Add Category. **Do not write a second implementation.** If that means extracting P048's logic into something shared, do that and say so.

**1.2** Report which parts you reused, and anything that had to differ.

A third copy of the same rule is a third place for it to drift.

---

# 2. The duplicate already exists — test against it

The series vocabulary appears to contain **both `Allied` and `ALLIED`**.

If so, this is the **first controlled vocabulary on this project where the near-duplicate is already real** rather than hypothetical. Division and Category were protected before they drifted. Series has already drifted.

**2.1** Confirm whether both genuinely exist as distinct series today, **with product counts for each**.

**2.2** **Do not merge or rename them in this prompt.** Report them. Merging two series with products behind them is a data change with a count consequence, and it belongs with P042.

**2.3** Use the pair as a test: adding `allied` must be refused and offered the existing value — and report **which of the two it offers**.

**That answer is genuinely ambiguous with both present, and the ambiguity is the finding.** Whatever it picks must be deliberate, not whichever row the query happened to return first.

---

# 3. Three things to establish first — read-only

**3.1** List every series in use, with its division and product count.

**3.2** **Is series scoped to a division, to a category, or global?** That decides where uniqueness is checked. Report what the schema actually says, not what seems sensible.

**3.3** **Does Sales map series through a fixed table, the way it maps divisions?**

Division resolves through `SOURCE_DIVISION_MAP` in the Sales application, and an unmapped value silently drops products from `mrp_master` and `mrp_history`. Series appears to be carried through **unmapped**, as `series_range` and `series`.

Confirm that. **If series is unmapped, the Sales warning from P048 Part 1 does not apply here and must not be copied across** — a warning that fires without cause trains people to ignore it. If series turns out to be mapped somewhere, say so; that changes this prompt.

**3.4** The field is read from a column headed `GROUP` in the Pipe & Fitting master and `RANGE` in the CP master. Report what the editor writes it into, and **whether those are the same field**.

## What the `Allied` / `ALLIED` pair implies

That vocabulary was already drifting **before any Add button existed**. The buttons make drift possible on purpose; they did not cause this.

Which is both an argument for the refusal being there, and a separate argument for someone eventually deciding which of those two is the real one. **That decision is not in this prompt** — it needs product counts on both sides and a ruling from Prayag, exactly as the duplicate Prince codes did under P034.
