# P053 — Step 3 — COMPETITION — Exclude a competitor, whole or by division or category

> **P053 Step 3** · app: **COMPETITION** (`prayag-price.com`)
> **THIS PROMPT CHANGES CODE.** It changes no product, price, competitor row or mapping.
> Quote `P053 Step 3` when reporting back.

A bucket list can today hold **products**. This adds **competitors** to the same list, so one saved list can say *"hide these 184 products, and ignore Apollo entirely"*.

---

# 1. The important difference — read this before building

**Excluding a product removes the whole comparison row.**
**Excluding a competitor does NOT.** It removes that competitor's prices from the comparison, and the Prayag product stays.

```
Excluded product   PW11          -> the PW11 row disappears
Excluded competitor Apollo       -> every row stays; Apollo's price is gone from each
```

**1.1** If a product's only competitor prices come from excluded competitors, **the row still appears**, with *"no competitor price under this filter"* in place of the comparison. **It is not dropped.** Dropping it would quietly change the product count, which is the failure this whole feature exists to prevent.

**1.2** Every aggregate recomputes from the remaining competitors: widest-gap rankings, positioning, coverage, by-brand and by-category breakdowns, and opportunity counts. **An excluded competitor must not appear in any total, any ranking or any average.** Hiding the rendered row while the totals still count it is wrong.

---

# 2. Three scopes

In the editor, a competitor rule is one row:

```
Competitor          Scope
Apollo              Everywhere
Prince              Pipes & Fittings                     (one or more divisions)
Ashirvad            SWR Fittings, Pipe Clip              (one or more categories)
```

**2.1 Everywhere** — that competitor never appears, in any division.

**2.2 By division** — that competitor's prices are hidden only on products in the chosen divisions. Multi-select.

**2.3 By category** — hidden only on products in the chosen categories. Multi-select. Category matching uses the **P051 normalised comparison**, so a rule on `Blaze 62` also catches `BLAZE 62`.

**2.4** A competitor may have more than one rule. The rules add up: Prince excluded in Pipes & Fittings *and* in CPVC Fittings means both.

**2.5** Competitors are chosen from **the stored competitor list**, never typed free-hand. That avoids Ashirvad / Ashirwad becoming two things.

---

# 3. Included lists

In an **Included** list, a competitor rule means *"show only these competitors"*. Same three scopes, inverted. If an Included list names no competitor at all, every competitor is shown — a list about products says nothing about competitors.

Same for the reverse: an Excluded list naming only competitors hides no products.

---

# 4. The editor

**4.1** A third tab in **2. Add items**, beside *Pick from the catalogue* and *Upload from Excel*:

```
Competitors
```

**4.2** It lists every competitor with a checkbox and, beside each, a scope selector defaulting to **Everywhere**. Choosing *By division* or *By category* opens a multi-select of the values that competitor actually has prices for — **do not offer divisions or categories where that competitor has no data**, and show the count:

```
Apollo        Everywhere            700 prices
Prince        By division  ▾        Pipes & Fittings (812)   CP Fittings / Faucets (133)
```

**4.3 The review panel states both parts plainly**, and updates live:

> *This list will hide **184** products and ignore **Apollo** entirely, and **Prince** in Pipes & Fittings. **1,512** competitor prices will not be counted.*

**4.4** Competitor rules appear in the item list as their own removable chips, visibly different from product entries.

---

# 5. The filter control and exports

**5.1** The active control's effect text carries both parts, still in one line:

```
● HO (Plumbing)   184 hidden · 2 competitors   ×
```

**5.2 The export disclosure names the competitor rules in full.** A file that quietly omits Apollo is exactly as misleading as one that quietly omits 184 products:

```
Showing: List name: "HO (Plumbing) — Excluded" · Type: Excluded ·
Effect: 184 items hidden from results; Apollo excluded everywhere;
Prince excluded in Pipes & Fittings
```

**5.3** Same screens as before: A–K only. L–T maintenance screens stay unfiltered and keep their notice.

# Tests

```
 1. Excluding a competitor everywhere removes its prices from every A-K
    screen and every aggregate, and removes no Prayag product row
 2. A product whose only competitor was excluded still appears, marked
    "no competitor price under this filter"
 3. Widest-gap, positioning, coverage and by-brand totals recompute;
    the excluded competitor appears in none of them
 4. A division-scoped rule affects only that division
 5. A category-scoped rule on "Blaze 62" also catches "BLAZE 62"
 6. Two rules on one competitor both apply
 7. An Included list naming no competitor shows all competitors
 8. An Excluded list naming only competitors hides no products
 9. The scope picker offers only divisions and categories where that
    competitor has prices
10. The export disclosure names every competitor rule in full, CSV and XLSX
11. The control's effect text shows products and competitors in one line
12. mrp_price_history count and checksum unchanged, both environments
13. Catalogue classification snapshot byte-identical, both environments
```

**Tests 2 and 3 are the ones that matter.** Everything else is convenience; a product silently vanishing, or a total still counting a competitor the user excluded, is a wrong number.

## Why this is worth having

Apollo's 700 prices are not loaded yet and its codes are hand-made. Ashirvad's list is eighteen months old and its pipe-clip prices are seven to seventeen times Prayag's, probably per pack. Until those are settled, anyone looking at a gap report is reading numbers that include known-bad data. One click to set a competitor aside is more honest than a mental note to ignore a column.
