## 2. The 301 variant matches — read this before using them

304 of the 2,066 unmapped production codes are the same code as an ERP entry differing only in
punctuation or case — `7991 F` / `7991-F`, `DB-52-3IN-N` / `DB-52-3 IN-N`, `QPR-22` / `Qpr-22`. Three
of those were the composites above; the remaining **301 are in the `variant_matches` sheet**.

**Zero of them are ambiguous** — no production code matches two ERP entries that disagree on division.
That was checked explicitly.

> ### ⚠️ These are listed PER CODE. They are not a normalisation rule and must not be generalised.
> Do **not** add punctuation-insensitive matching to the loader, the matcher, or anything else on the
> strength of this. `TTS-01` and `TTS - 01` are **different products** in this catalogue, as are
> `DB-02L` and `DB-02 L`. This batch happens to be clean; the general rule is not safe. P057 A.3(a)
> and P060 §0.2 still stand.

Coverage goes from 5,125 to about 5,429 of 7,191. The remaining ~1,762 production codes are genuinely
absent from the ERP file and **keep their current division** — silence in the mapping is not a
correction.

## 3. The 26 overrides — ERP rejected, current division kept

Of the 28 residual disagreements the validation surfaced, **26 are ERP classification noise.** They
are listed in the new `overrides` sheet with the reasoning on every row.

**`FR-36`, `FR-37`, `FR-40` → stay in `CP (QUAA / FERN)`**
The FERN line. 9 of the 12 `FR-` codes in the ERP are Item Type `QUAA`; these three are the only ones
typed `PTMT`. Three outliers against nine siblings reads as ERP noise, and production already has them
correctly in CP (QUAA / FERN).

**`MHC-01` … `MHC-10` → stay in `Pipes & Fittings`**
Manhole covers — a drainage product. The ERP types them `SANITARYWARE`, but production and the MRP
workbooks both place them in Pipes & Fittings, and a manhole cover is not ceramic sanitaryware. Two
sources against one, and the odd one out is the one furthest from the product.

**`I-151` … `I-175` (13 ball-cock codes) → stay in `PTMT & Plastic Fittings`**
Cistern internals. The ERP splits this single family across three Item Types — 11 `C P`, 2
`CP ACCESSORIES`, 2 `PTMT` — with identical "Ball Cock Rod Brass" items landing in two different
types. The ERP is internally inconsistent here; production and the older backfill are consistent on
PTMT. Splitting one product family 13/2 on a noisy signal is worse than leaving it alone.

**Accepted, not overridden — `492-W`, `493-W`** (ABS Soap Dispenser Oval / Round 400ml White) move
from PTMT to CP Fittings / Faucets. ERP Item Group is `CP ACCESSORIES`, and a soap dispenser is a
bathroom accessory. These are the 2 residual disagreements that remain by design.
