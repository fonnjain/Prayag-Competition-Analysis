## 1. What we are building

A **Price Calculator** page in the Price Finder app at route `/price-finder/calculator`.

It answers one question a salesperson asks every day: *"At my discount and their discount, for this
quantity, are we actually cheaper than them — and by how much?"*

MRP-to-MRP comparison already exists. This is **net-to-net at a real order quantity**, built one
product at a time, with a running total.

### The shape of it

The page is a list of **blocks**. One block = one of our products plus the competitors being compared
against it.

```
┌─ 1 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  OUR PRODUCT                                                                          │
│  [ 321 · P.V.C. Connection (Standard Series 1 Pc Packing) 12"/300mm      ▾ ]          │
│  MRP ₹92.00  (from 01-09-2026)   Disc [ 20.0 ]%   Net ₹73.60   Qty [ 1 ]  Total ₹73.60│
│                                                                                       │
│  COMPETITOR                                                                           │
│  [ Brand A ▾ ]  MRP ₹91.00 (from 01-07-2026)  Disc [ 18.0 ]%  Net ₹74.62              │
│                 Qty 1 (same as ours)          Total ₹74.62     ▲ 1.4% above us        │
│                                                                                       │
│  [ + Add competitor ]                                                      [ Remove ] │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

[ + Add product ]                                                     [ Final Summary ]
```

- **`+ Add competitor`** adds another competitor row **inside the same block**, compared against the
  same product.
- **`+ Add product`** starts a **new block**.
- **`Final Summary`** rolls every block up into one view.

## 2. Choosing the product

Two routes into the same thing, both on the block header. The user picks whichever suits them.

**Route A — direct.** One `SearchableSelect` over item code and product name. Typing `321` or
`pvc connection` filters. This is the fast path and should be the default focus.

**Route B — division → category → product.** Three cascading `SearchableSelect`s:

1. **Division** — from `GET /price-finder/browse`.
2. **Category** — **scoped to the chosen division** (the browse endpoint already does this correctly;
   do not use `/analysis/filters`, which does not). Resets when division changes.
3. **Product** — scoped to division + category. Resets when either changes.

Both routes write to the same block state. Switching route does not clear an already-chosen product.

**Variants.** If the chosen product has colour or price variants (the product endpoint returns
`variants`), show a variant `SearchableSelect` next to the product. Default to Standard. A variant
price is distinct price evidence — never silently use the standard price for a variant row, and never
mix the two.

## 3. The row model and the arithmetic

### Our row (one per block)

| Column | Source / behaviour |
|---|---|
| Item code | from the selected product, read-only |
| Product name | read-only, truncate with a tooltip |
| MRP | current approved price for the chosen variant, as of the page's as-of date. Show the effective date beneath it, small and muted |
| Discount % | editable. Seeded from `discount_settings` scope `"Our"` (see §6). `0`–`100`, up to 2 decimals |
| Net | `round(MRP × (1 − disc/100), 2)` — read-only, computed |
| Qty | editable. Integer ≥ 1. **Default 1** |
| Total | `round(Net × Qty, 2)` — read-only |

### Competitor rows (zero or more per block)

| Column | Source / behaviour |
|---|---|
| Competitor | `SearchableSelect`, **scoped to this product** — see §4 |
| MRP | that competitor's current price for this item code, with its own effective date beneath |
| Discount % | editable. Seeded from `discount_settings` scope = the competitor's name |
| Net | `round(MRP × (1 − disc/100), 2)` |
| Qty | **mirrors our qty, read-only.** Tooltip: *"Quantity follows the Prayag row so the comparison stays like-for-like."* |
| Total | `round(Net × Qty, 2)` |
| Position | `compNet − ourNet` as ₹ and %, with direction |

### Exact arithmetic — follow this, do not improvise

```
netUnit   = round(mrp * (1 - discountPct / 100), 2)      // round to paise at unit level
rowTotal  = round(netUnit * qty, 2)

diffUnit  = competitorNetUnit - ourNetUnit
diffPct   = ourNetUnit === 0 ? null : (diffUnit / ourNetUnit) * 100
diffTotal = competitorRowTotal - ourRowTotal
```

- Round at **unit** level first, then multiply by qty. That is what an invoice does; computing at full
  precision and rounding once at the end gives a different number and the sales team will notice.
- `diffPct` is relative to **our** net, matching the existing `priceChangePct` convention in
  `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/priceFinder.ts`.
- Display: `₹` with Indian digit grouping (`1,23,456.00`), two decimals, via
  `Intl.NumberFormat('en-IN', { style: 'currency', currency: 'INR' })`.
- **Position labels:** `diffUnit > 0.005` → *"X% above us"* (▲, amber); `diffUnit < -0.005` →
  *"X% below us"* (▼, red — they are undercutting us); `|diffUnit| ≤ 0.005` → *"At par"* (grey).

### Things that must never happen

- **No invented prices.** A missing competitor price is never `0` and never blank-as-zero. The
  competitor cannot be selected at all if there is no usable price (§4), so this should be
  unreachable — if it happens anyway, show `No price` and exclude the row from every total.
- **No silent basis mismatch.** If our price basis and the competitor's differ (per-piece vs per-pipe,
  MRP vs net, different tax treatment), show an amber ⚠ badge on the competitor row reading
  *"Different price basis — check before relying on this"*. Do not block it, do not hide it, and carry
  the flag into the export.
- **No competitor qty edit.** Mirrored, always.

## 4. The competitor dropdown

Use `GET /price-finder/competitors?itemCode=<code>&asOf=<date>` from **P057 Part C**.

- Only competitors with a **matched, non-null, currently-effective** price for *that item code* appear.
- A competitor already chosen in this block is removed from the list (no duplicates within a block).
- If the product has **no** competitor prices at all, replace `+ Add competitor` with the muted line
  *"No competitor prices recorded for this product."* and let the block stand on its own — it still
  contributes its value to the summary.
- Show the competitor's current effective date in the option row so the user can see how fresh it is.

## 5. Buttons and flow

| Control | Where | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `+ Add competitor` | bottom of each block | Appends a competitor row to **that** block. Disabled with a tooltip when no un-chosen competitors remain |
| `+ Add product` | bottom of the page | Appends a new empty block. Scrolls to it and focuses its product picker |
| `Remove` | per row and per block | Removes that row/block. Removing the our-row removes the block |
| `Final Summary` | bottom right, sticky | Opens the summary (§7). Disabled until at least one block has a product |
| `As of` | page header | Date input, as on the existing comparison page. Re-resolves every price. Default today |
| `Reset` | page header | Clears all blocks after a confirm dialog |
| `Export Excel` | in the summary | §7 |

**Limits.** Max **50 blocks** (matches the existing `/price-finder/comparison` cap) and max **10
competitor rows per block**. On hitting either, disable the button with an explanatory tooltip.

**Empty state.** On first load: one empty block, product picker focused, with a one-line explainer —
*"Pick a product, set your discount and quantity, then add the competitors you want to price against."*

## 6. Default discounts

Discount defaults come from the existing `discount_settings` table
(`lib/db/src/schema/catalog.ts:336` — `scope` text, `discount_pct` double, unique on `scope`).

Add **`GET /price-finder/discount-defaults`** returning `{ scope, discountPct }[]` for our scope plus
every competitor scope. Do **not** reuse `/analysis/discount-settings` — that route sits behind the
Analysis app's admin surface and seeds rows as a side effect.

- Seed each new row's discount from this, then let the user override it freely. An override is
  per-row and never written back to `discount_settings`.
- If a scope has no stored default, seed `0` and show the field with a muted *"no default set"* hint.
- Show a small line under the page header: *"Discounts pre-filled from saved settings. Edit any cell
  to override — nothing here changes the saved settings."*

### Access — DECIDED 16 Oct 2026: the calculator is open to everyone

**`/price-finder/calculator` has no sign-in gate.** Any visitor can open it and use it in full —
select products, enter discounts and quantities, add competitors, read the summary, export. This is
a counter and field tool; blocking it behind a login defeats the point.

The one thing that is **not** public is the *stored* discount defaults, because a trade-discount
table is the company's margin structure:

| Viewer | Calculator | Discount fields |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous | Full access | Start at `0`, typed in by hand. `GET /price-finder/discount-defaults` is **not called** |
| Signed in (admin / user / counter) | Full access | Pre-filled from `discount_settings`, then editable |

This is not an access control on the calculator — nobody is turned away, and every feature works
either way. A signed-in user simply gets the convenience of not retyping the standard discount, which
mirrors how the rest of Price Finder already behaves (current price public, price history behind
sign-in).

`GET /price-finder/discount-defaults` stays authenticated-only and returns 401 to an anonymous
caller. Do not add an anonymous fallback that returns zeros from that endpoint — just don't call it.

Show the hint line under the header only when signed in:
*"Discounts pre-filled from saved settings. Edit any cell to override — nothing here changes the
saved settings."* Anonymous visitors get:
*"Enter your discount and the competitor's to compare net prices."*

## 7. Final Summary

Opens as a full-width panel below the blocks (not a modal — it must be printable and scrollable).

### 7.1 Per-product table

One row per block:

| Item code | Product | Qty | Our net total | *Brand A* | *Brand B* | Best competitor | Gap vs best |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- One column per competitor **that appears in at least one block**.
- A cell is `—` where that competitor was not selected for that product. `—` is **not zero** and must
  be excluded from that column's total, not counted as nil.
- *Best competitor* = the lowest competitor net total in that block. *Gap vs best* = our total minus
  that, as ₹ and %, coloured by the §3 rules.

### 7.2 Overall totals

```
Products compared          12
Total quantity             340
Our net value              ₹ 2,48,610.00

Brand A      compared on  9 of 12 products   ₹ 1,92,440.00   vs our ₹ 1,88,900.00 on the same 9   ▲ 1.9% above us
Brand B      compared on  4 of 12 products   ₹   62,180.00   vs our ₹   64,050.00 on the same 4   ▼ 2.9% below us
```

**The denominator discipline is the whole point of this section.** A competitor's total must only ever
be compared against **our total over the same subset of products**. Never put a competitor's 9-product
total next to our 12-product total. State the subset size on every line.

### 7.3 Position counts

Per competitor, over the products where it was selected:

```
Brand A      we are cheaper on 6   at par on 1   higher on 2
```

### 7.4 Disclosures — always shown, never suppressed

- *"N products in this set have no competitor selected."* (omit when zero)
- *"N products have no competitor price recorded at all."* (omit when zero)
- *"Prices resolved as of DD-MM-YYYY."* — always
- *"N rows have a different price basis from ours — see the ⚠ marks."* (omit when zero)
- The active bucket-list / head-office scope line, in the same wording the comparison page already
  uses (see §9)

### 7.5 Export

`Export Excel` produces one workbook with two sheets, following the pattern already in
`priceFinder.ts` (`XLSX.utils.json_to_sheet` → `book_append_sheet` → buffer → `Content-Disposition`):

- **Sheet `Calculator detail`** — one row per competitor row: item code, product name, division,
  category, variant, as-of date, our MRP, our effective date, our discount %, our net, qty, our total,
  competitor, competitor MRP, competitor effective date, competitor discount %, competitor net,
  competitor total, difference ₹, difference %, position, price-basis flag.
- **Sheet `Summary`** — §7.2 and §7.3, plus every disclosure line from §7.4 as text rows.

Filename: `prayag-price-calculator-<asOf>.xlsx`.

## 9. Rules this page must obey

These are the app's existing commitments. The calculator does not get to opt out of them.

1. **Current price means currently effective**, not the newest row uploaded. Reuse the existing
   current-price resolution — do not write a new one, and do not read `is_current` as the source of
   truth.
2. **Respect bucket-list scope.** Head-office enforcement first, then the user's active Included
   scope, then their Excluded scopes — exactly as the browse and comparison screens do. The product
   picker, the competitor picker and the summary all sit inside that scope, and the active scope is
   disclosed on the page and in the export.
3. **The calculator is read-only.** It writes nothing: not prices, not discount settings, not
   mappings, not bucket lists. It is a calculator, not an editor.
4. **Coverage is disclosed, never papered over.** §7.4 lines are not optional.
5. **No new competitor name may be typed in.** Competitors come from stored data only.

## 10. Acceptance tests

Write these as real tests, not a manual checklist:

1. Product with MRP ₹92.00, disc 20%, qty 1 → net ₹73.60, total ₹73.60. Qty 7 → total ₹515.20.
2. **Unit-first rounding, the case that actually discriminates.** MRP ₹92.00, disc 33.33%, qty 7:
   - `netUnit = round(92 × 0.6667, 2) = round(61.3364, 2) = 61.34`
   - `rowTotal = round(61.34 × 7, 2) = 429.38`  ← **this is the expected value**
   - computing in one step gives `round(92 × 0.6667 × 7, 2) = round(429.3548, 2) = 429.35`, which is
     ₹0.03 out. Assert `429.38`, and add a comment saying why, so nobody "simplifies" it later.
3. Competitor at ₹91.00, disc 18%, qty mirrors 7 → net ₹74.62, total ₹522.34, position *"1.4% above
   us"* (`(74.62 − 73.60) / 73.60 = 1.386%`, against the test-1 our-row).
4. Changing our qty to 3 changes the competitor row's qty to 3 without a second user action.
5. A product with no competitor prices renders the muted no-competitors line and still contributes its
   value to the overall total.
6. Summary with Brand A on 9 of 12 products compares against our 9-product subtotal, and the
   *"compared on 9 of 12"* text is present.
7. A competitor is never offered for a product it has no current price for.
8. Discount `-5`, `101`, and non-numeric input are all rejected at the field with a message; qty `0`
   and `1.5` likewise.
9. Export detail sheet row count equals the number of competitor rows across all blocks.
10. Changing the as-of date re-resolves every price and every total.
