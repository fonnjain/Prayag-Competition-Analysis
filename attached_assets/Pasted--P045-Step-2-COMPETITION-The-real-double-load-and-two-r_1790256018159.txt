# 3. `APUGD007` is reserved — but reserved for what?

Dropping it was right: no price, no row. But **a reserved number with no owner is an identity waiting to be reused.**

**Bind the reservation to the product, not to the number.** `APUGD007` belongs to that specific UGD item — identified by normalised description and size — and to nothing else.

- If Apollo later prices that product, it takes `APUGD007` back.
- If a different product would be assigned `APUGD007`, that must **fail**.

Report how the binding is stored, and prove it: attempt to mint `APUGD007` for a different product and confirm it is refused.
