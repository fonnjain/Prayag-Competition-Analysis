# Replit Prompt — Price Finder Comparison, as a Separate Surface

A comparison page that belongs to **Price Finder**, not to Competition Analysis. Separate because the two serve different people with different access.

`comparison.tsx` in `prayag-product-db` stays exactly as it is. This is a second, narrower surface — do not merge them, and do not change the existing one.

---

## Why separate

Competition Analysis is a management tool. It exposes net-to-net pricing driven by discount settings, match confidence internals, mapping review, unmapped competitor products and import controls. **Margin data is in there.**

Price Finder is a counter tool. Someone serving a customer needs the MRP, the competitor's MRP and the difference. Nothing else.

Building the selected-set comparison into the existing page would mean granting counter staff access to a surface carrying discount and margin information. A separate page is what makes the access boundary possible.

---

## Part A — A third role

`users.role` is `varchar` defaulting to `'user'`, and `requireAdmin` tests `role !== "admin"`. Two roles today: `admin` and `user`.

Add a **`counter`** role — read-only, and scoped to price lookup:

| Surface | admin | user | counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price Finder | yes | yes | **yes** |
| Price Finder Comparison (this page) | yes | yes | **yes** |
| Product Database / catalogue browse | yes | yes | no |
| Competition Analysis | yes | yes | **no** |
| Discount settings, net-to-net | yes | no | **no** |
| Imports, MRP load, mapping review | yes | no | no |

Confirm the `user` row above against how the application is actually used before implementing — I have inferred it. If `user` should also be restricted from Competition Analysis, say so and propose it rather than assuming either way.

**Enforce server-side at the router**, using the existing `requireAdmin` pattern. A hidden nav item is not access control. A `counter` session requesting a Competition Analysis endpoint directly must receive 403.

Add role assignment to the existing admin users page, and make the role visible there.

---

## Part B — Selection in Price Finder

A checkbox on each search result and each catalogue-browse row. Selecting one or more reveals a bar with the count and a **Compare** button.

The selection must survive a new search, moving between search and browse, and opening a product detail page and returning. Someone looks up a bib cock, selects it, searches a sink cock, selects that — both stay.

Include a **Clear** control and the ability to remove an individual product from the bar without finding its row again.

Cap at **50 products**, with a clear message at the limit.

---

## Part C — The comparison page

A new route on the Price Finder surface. It shows only the selected products.

Per row:

- Cat No., product name, series, division
- **Prayag MRP in force**, with its effective date
- **Each competitor's MRP in force**, with its effective date
- The difference, and which is cheaper
- Where either side has a future price: the upcoming price and the date

**Both sides must resolve at the same date.** Never the current price of one against a dated price of the other — that mismatch produced a false +10.8% median on this project and took a week to unwind. Reuse the shared period-resolution logic; do not write a second implementation.

### What this page must not show

- Net-to-net pricing, discount percentages, or anything derived from discount settings
- Match confidence, match method, or review status
- Unmapped competitor products
- Cost, margin, or any internal field

If a field is not needed to answer *"what do we charge and what do they charge"*, leave it off. This page will be open on a counter in front of customers.

### Competitor filter

Multi-select, so the user chooses which competitor columns appear. Only Sparsh Pearl has data today; Vectus exists with none.

A competitor with no price for a product must show as **"no data"**, visually distinct from a difference of zero. Those are different facts and must not look alike.

### Shareable

Pass the selection as product codes in the URL so the view can be sent to a colleague — that is how this will be used. If the list outgrows a URL, fall back to a stored selection with an id, rather than losing shareability.

---

## Part D — Export

An Excel export of the comparison as shown, honouring the competitor filter.

Every price must carry its **effective date** in the sheet, and the sheet must state the as-of date the comparison was run for. These files get mailed and read weeks later; a price without a date cannot be checked.

Same exclusions as the page — no net-to-net, no margin, no internal fields. A counter export may reach a customer.

## Acceptance tests

```
1.  A counter-role session can open Price Finder and this comparison page.
2.  A counter-role session requesting a Competition Analysis endpoint directly
    receives 403, not a hidden menu item.
3.  A counter-role session requesting discount settings receives 403.
4.  Selecting 3 products across two separate searches, then Compare, shows
    exactly those 3.
5.  The selection survives a new search, browsing, and product detail and back.
6.  The comparison URL opens the same set in a new session.
7.  Prayag and competitor prices in each row resolve at the same date; a product
    with no competitor price shows "no data", not a zero difference.
8.  The export contains an effective date beside every price and the as-of date
    on the sheet.
9.  No net-to-net, discount, margin, match-confidence or review field appears on
    the page or in the export.
10. The existing Competition Analysis comparison page is unchanged in behaviour
    for admin and user roles.
11. No catalog_products row is created by any part of this flow.
```

Tests 2, 3 and 9 are the point of building this separately. If a counter role can reach margin data by any route, the separation has failed.
