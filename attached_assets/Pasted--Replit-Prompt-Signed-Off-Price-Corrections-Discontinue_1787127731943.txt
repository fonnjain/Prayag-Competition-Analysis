## 3. Part B — 150 discontinued products

The official files mark withdrawn products with a literal `REMOVED` in the new-price column. A scan of all six files found **239 such marker cells resolving to 150 distinct codes**:

| Division | Codes |
|---|---:|
| CP Fittings / Faucets | 109 |
| PTMT & Plastic Fittings | 33 |
| Pipes & Fittings | 8 |

This is much wider than the 9 PTMT sinks identified earlier. The danger is that for many of these the price column **still holds a carried-over calculated figure**, so they look like ordinary priced products — a wrong-but-plausible number that passes visual review, unlike ₹0.05.

### B1. Schema

In `lib/db/src/schema/catalog.ts`, add to `catalogProductsTable`:

```ts
discontinuedFrom: date("discontinued_from"),
```

Nullable; `null` means not scheduled for withdrawal. Then `pnpm --filter @workspace/db run push`.

Setting `is_active = false` instead would be wrong — these products are legitimately on sale until their division's revision date. The mechanism has to be date-effective.

### B2. Apply

For each of the 150 codes: set `discontinued_from` to the effective date of the revision that withdrew it — **01 Sep 2026** for PTMT, **01 Aug 2026** for CP, **01 Feb 2026** for Pipes & Fittings — and delete any `mrp_price_history` row dated on or after that date. Earlier price rows stay untouched.

Pipes & Fittings is already past its date, so those 8 take effect immediately. Report them separately so the user sees what disappears today.

### B3. Respect the flag at query time

A product is live at `:asOf` when `discontinued_from IS NULL OR discontinued_from > :asOf`. Apply at every site that resolves a current price. These exist today — verify the list is still complete rather than assuming:

- `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/catalog.ts` — `recomputeCurrentFlags()`
- `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/analysis.ts` (the `DISTINCT ON (item_code)` CTEs)
- `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/comparison.ts`
- `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/priceFinder.ts`
- `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/catalogImportExport.ts`

A discontinued product must still resolve for an `asOf` before its `discontinued_from`, so historical comparisons stay reproducible.

### B4. Surface it

Contract-first: add `discontinuedFrom` (nullable date) to the product schemas in `lib/api-spec/openapi.yaml`, then `pnpm --filter @workspace/api-spec run codegen`. Never hand-edit `lib/api-hooks` or `lib/api-zod`.

Show a badge on any product with a future `discontinuedFrom`: `Discontinued from 01 Sep 2026`. The sales team needs to see a withdrawal coming, not discover it on the day.

## 5. Part D — prevent recurrence

Add a post-load validation pass to the MRP loader that **reports, never silently drops**, any row where:

1. MRP is below ₹50 in PTMT, CP or Sanitaryware.
2. Period-over-period change is above +60% or below −20%.
3. MRP is below ₹1 in any division.
4. The source cell for the new price contains a non-numeric marker such as `REMOVED`, `NEW`, or `On the Way`.

Rule 4 is the one that would have caught both defects in this document. Surface results on the **Load MRP** page as a blocking review step: the load completes, flagged rows are listed with old value, new value and reason, and the user confirms or cancels before rows become current. Do not auto-reject — this project keeps a human in the loop on price data.
