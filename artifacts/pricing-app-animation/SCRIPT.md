# Pricing App — Animated Walkthrough (16:9, 33.0 s)

## Treatment
- Promise: one app turns 6,157 product prices and every competitor quote into a defensible pricing decision.
- Identity: the screenshots are neutral grayscale (branding removed), so the film frames them on a warm "ledger paper" field (#EFE9DD) with deep ink (#16201E) and a single signal-vermilion accent (#E2522B) used only to point at evidence. Display: Bricolage Grotesque. Data/labels: JetBrains Mono.
- Hero: the real app screens, treated as physical sheets under a virtual camera.
- Motion: (1) 3D sheet tilt + camera punch-in on a specific UI region, (2) vermilion "evidence ring" stroke-traced around the exact cell/KPI, (3) masked kinetic type with oversized numerals.
- Arc: hook number -> catalogue -> market comparison -> dashboard -> MRP generator -> Price Finder -> all sheets fan into one wall -> lockup.
- Payoff: the finale is the only wide shot where every screen is visible at once, ending on a type lockup on ink.
